## 2. Bridging the 15-Year Gap: `evemu_Crucible` (2011) vs. Live V24.01 Client (2026)

### 2.1 Dual-Bus RPC Architecture (`machoNet` + Quasar `publicGatewaySvc`)
In 2011 (`evemu_Crucible`), 100% of client-server communication flowed over `machoNet` (`eveMarshal` binary streams on TCP 26000).
In the live **V24.01 client (`build 3619879`)**, CCP operates a **hybrid dual-bus architecture**:
1. **Classic `machoNet` (`sm.RemoteSvc` / `sm.ProxySvc` / `ConnectToRemoteService` / `Moniker`):** Powers core simulation, session state, stations/structures, inventory, Dogma, Destiny space flight, market orders, contracts, agents, fleet, corporation/alliance management, and wormholes (**193 active remote services** in V24.01).
2. **Modern Quasar `publicGatewaySvc` (`eve_public.*` Protobufs):** Powers newer UI systems via `sm.GetService('publicGatewaySvc').send_character_request(request_proto, response_cls)` and `subscribe_to_notice(...)` across **135 Protobuf domains** (`69` `eve_public.*` public gateway domains + `66` `eve.*` internal domains across `1,455` `_pb2.pyj` modules):
   - **The Agency & Unified Job Board** (`jobboard/`, `agent/`, `opportunity/`)
   - **AIR Career Program & Daily Goals** (`career/`, `dailygoal/`)
   - **Corporation & Freelance Projects** (`freelance/`, `corporationgoal/`)
   - **Skill Plans & Certificates** (`skill/`)
   - **Paragon Hub, Ship Cosmetics & SKINR** (`cosmetic/`)
   - **Sovereignty Skyhooks / Upwell Equinox Suites** (`sovereignty/`)
   - **PLEX Vault & NES Store** (`plex/`, `entitlement/`, `store/`)

Because `EvoMu`'s Python Client Bridge (`client-bridge/evomu_bridge.py`) hooks `PublicGatewayShim` (the **4th Chokepoint**) right alongside `sm.RemoteSvc` and `sm.ScatterEvent`, `EvoMu` can intercept and serve both `machoNet` RPCs and Quasar Protobuf requests over the same multiplexed connection.

## 4. Authoritative Data Sources for Missions, Quests, Dungeons & World Content

A major finding from inspecting the installed V24.01 client (`resfileindex.txt`) is that **CCP ships the complete binary static data for Missions, Epic Arcs, Dungeons, Node Graphs, Conversations, and Solar System Content directly inside `res:/staticdata/`**:

1. **`res:/staticdata/missions.fsdbinary` (`1.28 MB`, schema in `missionsLoader.so`):**
   - Contains every agent mission definition: `nameID`, `descriptionID`, `missionBriefingID`, `agentTypeID`, `corporationID`, `divisionID`, `factionID`, `careerID`, `level`, `isImportant`, `expirationTime`, `fixedLpRewardAlpha`, `fixedLpRewardOmega`, `nodeGraphID`, `clientObjectives`, `missionRewards`, `extraRewards`, `courierMission`, and `killMission`.
2. **`res:/staticdata/dungeons.fsdbinary` (`52.3 MB`, schema in `dungeonsLoader.so`):**
   - Contains every mission pocket, cosmic anomaly, combat escalation, and Abyssal room: `dungeonID`, `dungeonNameID`, `archetypeID`, `difficulty`, `entryObjectID`, `connections` (acceleration gates between rooms), `standingRestrictions`, `shipRestrictions`, and `rooms` (with exact 3D coordinates `x, y, z`, `yaw, pitch, roll`, `radius`, `entities`, `behaviorName`, `triggerSpawn`, `aggressionRange`, `orbitRange`, `orbitVelocity`).
3. **`res:/staticdata/epicarcs.fsdbinary` (`16.6 KB`, schema in `epicArcsLoader.so`):**
   - Contains all **9 Epic Arcs** (SOE *The Blood-Stained Stars*, Caldari *Penumbra*, Amarr *Right to Rule*, Gallente *Syndication*, Minmatar *Wildfire*, Guristas *Smash and Grab*, Angel *Angel Sound*, ORE *Fractured Legacy*, and AIR *Vision of Greatness*) with branching mission DAGs (`agentID`, `nextMissions`, `failMissionID`, `chapterTitleID`, `arcRestartInterval`).
4. **`res:/staticdata/nodegraphs.fsdbinary` (`8.25 MB`) & `res:/staticdata/objectivechains.fsdbinary` (`239 KB`):**
   - Contains the modern state-machine node graphs that drive the AIR New Player Experience (NPE), Career Agents, Dungeon room triggers, and UI objective chains.
5. **`res:/staticdata/conversations.fsdbinary` (`319 KB`) & `res:/staticdata/dialogs.static` (`196 KB` SQLite3):**
   - Contains NPC agent dialogue trees, voiceover cues, and interactive mission conversation prompts.
6. **`res:/staticdata/solarsystemcontent.static` (`92.3 MB` SQLite3), `systems.static`, `jumps.static`, `blueprints.static`, `clonegrades.static`, `dbuffcollections.static`, `fighterabilities.static`:**
   - Standard **SQLite 3** databases containing all solar systems, stargate jumps, planets, moons, asteroid belts, NPC stations, blueprints, Alpha/Omega clone skill caps, and fighter abilities.
7. **`res:/localizationfsd/localization_fsd_en-us.pickle` (`25.8 MB`):**
   - Resolves every integer `nameID`, `descriptionID`, and `missionBriefingID` into English text.
8. **Complementary Public Community Sources:**
   - **EVE University Wiki (`wiki.eveuniversity.org/Mission_reports`):** Detailed player-verified wave trigger notes, EWAR mechanics, and blitz conditions for Level 1–5 security/storyline/epic arc missions.
   - **Fuzzwork SDE (`fuzzwork.co.uk/dump/`) & Everef (`everef.net`):** Pre-converted SQLite/CSV dumps of CCP's public YAML SDE (`invTypes`, `dgmTypeAttributes`, `dgmTypeEffects`, `dgmExpressions`, `agtAgents`, `staStations`, `mapDenormalize`).
   - **Hoboleaks (`hoboleaks.space`):** Historical diff archives of `fsdbinary` changes across Tranquility patches.
